isometrica lab

What the world's generators make, to look at and listen to without a game: everything here is painted or played in the page itself, from the same code the games use - nothing is a file made beforehand.

Sprites Every picture the generators paint - ground, roads, buildings, trees, vehicles, stones, smoke - a generator at a time, lit or at night. Sounds Thunder, horns, sirens, and the forest, the sea and the city to loop under the game. Music Slow jazz for each place, by day and by night, played the way a game would: the weather fades it from clear to rain to storm in time. Woods map Where the world grows its trees, and which, from above: spruce forest, pine and birch groves, broadleaf woods, alder by the water and open meadow, shading into each other. Traffic testbed The game's own traffic on roads of your choosing, from above: put cars, trucks and buses on where you like, going where you like, follow one, and see what goes wrong - turning round mid-road, standing too long, locking up. Lighting bench A house lit by its windows and the street, the ways of lighting it side by side.

# city-sim design

What the city builder is meant to become, and what has been settled on the
way there. The game's README says what it is today; this says where it is
going and why.

Every point is marked:

- **Done**: in the game.
- **Phase 0**: next, for the single-player game, made so that it still holds
  once the world is shared.
- **Later**: planned, not started.
- **Open**: not settled yet.

## What it is after

- Seamless and intuitive, realistic where that is fun, the boring parts
  simplified away.
- No borders and no land to buy: the city is held together by its roads,
  the arteries everything else hangs off. A town can stay compact or grow a
  remote rural part; both are valid ways to play.
- Original, and ready for many players' cities on one world, without
  borders to share it out.
